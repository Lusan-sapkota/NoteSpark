Notes that stay on your devices

NoteSpark is a calm notes app for Android and your computer. Write in plain text or Markdown, keep tasks inside your notes, and find anything by what it means. There's no account and no cloud: everything stays on your device.

What it does

Quick to write in, calm to read, and nothing you'd miss from a cloud notes app. Scroll to see each part on a computer, a tablet and a phone.

  1. Write

    • Plain text or Markdown
      Pick per note, with a preview. Headings, lists and links are on the toolbar.
    • Undo, redo and history
      Every save keeps the version before, with what changed marked.

  2. Tasks

    • Tasks inside your notes
      Start a line with - [ ]. Add due:2026-11-01 for a reminder, or every:week to repeat it.

  3. Find

    • Search by meaning
      A small model on your device finds notes about the same idea, even when the words differ.
    • Links and tags
      Link notes with [[Note title]]; renaming a note updates its links. Filter Home by #tag.
    • Find in a note
      Jump between matches in long notes, straight from a search on Home.
    • Rediscover
      One older note a day on Home, written on this date or close to what you're working on.

  4. Private by design

    • No internet requests
      No accounts, analytics, crash reports or update checks. Optional sync between your own devices stays on your own network.
    • Profiles with a PIN or fingerprint
      Keep Personal and Work apart. Locked profiles are encrypted on your device.
    • Locks again when you leave
      After a minute in the background, a locked profile asks for its PIN.

  5. Never trapped

    • A scratchpad
      One per profile for quick thoughts. File a line as a note when it's ready, or clear it.
    • Markdown in and out
      Export one note or all of them as Markdown. Import Markdown, text or zip files from other apps.
    • Automatic backups
      Daily or weekly to a folder you pick, keeping the last five.
    • Quick capture
      On Android: share text from any app, long-press the icon for New note, or use the home-screen widget.

Nothing leaves your devices

NoteSpark makes no network requests of its own. The one exception is an image from a web address you add to a note, and only if you turn on Show web images in Settings (off by default): on Android it's downloaded once and kept on your device. Optional sync between your own devices stays on your own network.
