Notes that stay on your phone

NoteSpark is a calm notes app for Android. Write in plain text or Markdown, keep tasks inside your notes, and find anything by what it means. There's no account and no cloud: everything stays on your device.

  • No account to create
  • No ads, analytics or tracking
  • Works without an internet connection
  • Search by meaning runs on the phone

What it does

Quick to write in, calm to read, and nothing you'd miss from a cloud notes app.

Write

  • Plain text or Markdown
    Pick per note, with a preview. Headings, lists and links are on the toolbar.
  • Tasks inside your notes
    Start a line with - [ ]. Add due:2026-11-01 for a reminder, or every:week to repeat it.
  • A scratchpad
    One per profile for quick thoughts. File a line as a note when it's ready, or clear it.
  • Undo, redo and history
    Every save keeps the version before, with what changed marked.

Find

  • Search by meaning
    A small model on the phone finds notes about the same idea, even when the words differ.
  • Links and tags
    Link notes with [[Note title]]; renaming a note updates its links. Filter Home by #tag.
  • Find in a note
    Jump between matches in long notes, straight from a search on Home.
  • Rediscover
    One older note a day on Home, written on this date or close to what you're working on.

Private by design

  • No network requests
    No sync servers, analytics, crash reports or update checks.
  • Profiles with a PIN or fingerprint
    Keep Personal and Work apart. Locked profiles are encrypted on the phone.
  • Locks again when you leave
    After a minute in the background, a locked profile asks for its PIN.

Never trapped

  • Markdown in and out
    Export one note or all of them as Markdown. Import Markdown, text or zip files from other apps.
  • Automatic backups
    Daily or weekly to a folder you pick, keeping the last five.
  • Quick capture
    Share text from any app, long-press the icon for New note, or use the home-screen widget.

Nothing leaves your phone

NoteSpark makes no network requests of its own. The one exception is an image from a web address you add to a note: it's downloaded once and kept on your phone.
